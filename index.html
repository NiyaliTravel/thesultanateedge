Something worth the wait

The Sultanate Edge is being prepared for its first guests. We are not taking bookings yet — dates, rooms and details will be announced here first.

Hinnavaru sits in Lhaviyani — known traditionally as Faadhippolhu — one of the Maldives' great diving atolls: fast channel drifts, the famous Shipyard wrecks and turtle-rich reefs, about 120 km north of Malé.

Good to know

Questions we are asked

Is The Sultanate Edge open yet?

Not yet. The house is being prepared for its first guests and is not taking bookings.

When does it open?

An opening date has not been announced. Dates, rooms and details will be published on this site first.

Where will it be?

On the rim of Lh. Hinnavaru, Lhaviyani Atoll, Maldives — where the island meets open water. Lhaviyani, known traditionally as Faadhippolhu, is one of the great diving atolls of the Maldives.

Where can I stay in the collection in the meantime?

The Sultanate Fulhadhoo (B. Fulhadhoo, Baa Atoll) and The Sultanate Courtyard (Lh. Hinnavaru, Lhaviyani Atoll) are open now. The Sultanate Laamu opens on 1 November 2026.

How will I hear when it opens?

Leave your details on this page, or write to stay@thesultanatehotels.com and we will let you know first.
